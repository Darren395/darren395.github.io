Academic Project · Semester 3

Toko Cris was developed as part of my third-semester coursework in Data and Information Management. The project explores how data can be structured and managed to support an e-commerce business while connecting the underlying information structure with practical system interfaces.

Project Overview

Toko Cris represents an e-commerce store that sells electronic products. The project focuses on organizing business information related to customers, orders, products, tracking, and promotional programs. The work includes normalization, entity and relationship definition, attribute and domain design, key identification, and interface concepts for sales and inventory management.

Reflection

This semester 3 project strengthened my understanding of how business information connects with database design and system requirements. It helped me move from identifying business entities and relationships to structuring them into a more detailed logical model and supporting the resulting information through practical interfaces.
