Contoso Retail Performance Analysis (2016–2025)

👤 Solo Project, My Role: Data Modeler & Dashboard Developer

A full relational data model built in Excel Power Pivot to analyze 10 years of retail sales data, covering revenue, profit, and order patterns across product categories, regions, and time. The model powers a set of DAX measures, PivotTables, PivotCharts, and an interactive dashboard with slicers and a timeline for exploring performance by year, category, and channel.

Key Insights

  • Total revenue reached $218.8M across 93,470 orders between 2016 and 2025, with a total profit of $122.3M (a 55.9% profit margin) and an average order value of $2,341.
  • Computers was by far the strongest category, generating $94.3M in revenue (43% of the total) and $52.7M in profit — more than double the next-highest category.
  • North America accounted for $135.1M in revenue (62% of the total), well ahead of Europe ($70.3M) and Australia ($13.4M).
  • 2023 was the peak year with $43.8M in revenue and 78.9% year-over-year growth, while 2024 and 2025 both declined (-27.8% and -7.5%).
  • Among the top 10 products, the Adventure Works Desktop PC2.33 XD233 (Silver) led with $1.93M in revenue.

Problem: Contoso's retail management wants to understand which product categories, regions, and time periods drive the most revenue and profit across its 10-year sales history, to guide inventory, marketing, and expansion decisions.

Recommendation: Prioritize inventory and marketing investment in the Computers category, since it drives both the highest revenue and the highest profit by a wide margin. Continue focusing core resources on North America given its dominant revenue share, while exploring targeted growth initiatives in Australia, currently the smallest region. Investigate what drove the strong 2023 performance to see if those tactics can be repeated, and review what caused the 2024–2025 slowdown. Treat 2016 and 2026 as partial periods when reporting year-over-year figures, since neither reflects a full year of data.

Data source: This project uses the Contoso Retail Dataset, a synthetic dataset built by Microsoft specifically for Business Intelligence training, generated with SQLBI's Contoso Data Generator and distributed publicly via GitHub. It is not real company data. The dataset includes about 224,000 sales transactions, 105,000 customers, 2,500 products, and 74 stores, spanning January 2016 to December 2025.

Data model: A star schema with one fact table (Sales) and four dimension tables (Customer, Product, Store, Date), connected through relationships in Power Pivot.

Approach: Raw columns were cleaned and trimmed in Power Query before loading into the Data Model. Relationships were built between Sales and each dimension table, and the Date table was marked as the official date table to enable time-intelligence calculations. DAX measures were then built for revenue, cost, profit, margin, order counts, and year-over-year growth, which power the PivotTables, PivotCharts, KPI cards, and slicers in the final dashboard.
